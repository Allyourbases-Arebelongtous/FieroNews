Nov 12-2016 NIFE Fall Meeting
 fieronews on October 28 2016
NIFE Fall Meeting - Kick Off Event - Grayslake, IL
Looking for a great winter project for your Fiero? Join NIFE at Rock Chevrolet in Grayslake, IL as we learn about restoring and enhancing the Fiero interior. We will have a Fiero in the shop to allow us to go through a live demonstration of recovering a headliner. We also plan to discuss ways to repair warped center consoles and shift surrounds, interior lighting options that are available, how to repair torn or ripped seats, and replacing the speakers and the grill cloth covers. We will also review the current vendors that are selling parts to make your Fiero interior look better than new.
The meeting will take place Saturday, November 12, 2016 starting at 10:00am at Rock Chevrolet, located at 1000 E. Belvidere Rd, Grayslake IL. After the meeting feel free to join us for lunch just down the road at Sammies located at 799 E Belvidere Rd, Grayslake, IL.

NIFE Fall Color Tour & NIFEs 25th Anniversary Dinner Party
 fieronews on September 22 2016
This years Fall Color Tour will be a break from the normal routine as this year we will include two events in one day: our 2016 Fall Color Tour and NIFEs 25th anniversary dinner celebration. The days events will begin at the McDonalds located at 1019 Maple Ave in Lisle, IL 60532. We will plan to DEPART from here at 1:00pm. If you would like to have lunch prior to our departure, please arrive accordingly to be ready to leave at 1:00pm.
Heading South on I-355, we will exit onto I-80 West over to the Larkin Avenue exit. From here we will get on US-6 to head into Channahon, IL to meet up with our hosts, NIFE Member Hank Roe and friend of NIFE Roger Gerken (you may recall them as they planned out our 2016 Spring Driving Tour as well) at the Channahon Parkway State Park. We will plan to meet here around 2:00pm.
Our first stop on this years Fall Color Tour will be the I&M Canal Park in Channahon, IL where our club will see an example of some amazing engineering dating back to the 1840s. To cross the Du Page River the builders had two choices, build a long and expensive aqueduct to carry the canal boats across the river or build a causeway creating a deep pool so the boats could cross using the river. They chose a causeway that the Du Page River cascades down on its way to the Illinois River. The Park also has one of only two surviving Lock Masters houses on the western part of the I&M.
The next stop, after driving down into the Illinois Valley, is the Aux Able lock and aqueduct that crosses a stream too small for a causeway. The other Lockmasters house is in this little park. Our group will then travel on one of the old trails into Morris, IL. The fall colors should be very nice. In Morris, just south of the Grundy County Courthouse, is the Morris I&M Canal Park. One of the features we will see here is a reproduction of a canal boat. On leaving Morris we will take IL Route 47 North to head to our NIFE 25th anniversary dinner, which will take place at Claddagh Irish Pub located at 1702 Commons Drive in Geneva, IL 60134. Check out their website located at www.claddaghirishpubs.com/geneva/ If you are not able to join us on this years Fall Color Tour, join us at Claddaghs Irish Pub around 5:00pm for a dinner celebration honoring NIFEs 25th anniversary.

Heartland Fiero Show 9-3-2016
 fieronews on August 23 2016
Want to attend a unique car show in a historic location? Come join us on Saturday, September 3, 2016 at the Heartland Fieros 13th Car Show held in the historic Village Of East Davenport, IA along the Mississippi River near Davenport, IA. The six square block business area has numerous restaurants, a great ice cream parlor and deli called Lagomarcinos that will feature the Fiero Freeze as well as many other gift and specialty shops. Davenport, IA is a short 2-1/2 hour drive from the Chicago area and well worth the trip. Following the car show we are invited to attend a barbeque at the home of the Heartland Fiero Clubs Chairman, Fred Bartemeyer. Show registration is from 10:00am - noon and awards will be at 3:30pm. For more information on the Village of East Davenport check out their website at villageofeastdavenport.com. The host hotel is again the Quality Inn & Suites in Davenport, IA located at 6605 North Brady Street. Reservations are being taken at 563-386-8336. If you are in town Friday night, Heartland Fieros hosts a group dinner; meet at the host hotel around 5:30pm. We look forward to seeing you in Davenport, IA for the show!
