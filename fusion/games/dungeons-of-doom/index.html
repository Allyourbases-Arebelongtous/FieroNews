DUNGEON COMMANDS & CONTROLS

Movement & Combat

  • Arrow Keys / Numpad : Move orthogonal & diagonal
  • H J K L : Left, Down, Up, Right
  • Y U B N : Diagonal movement
  • Walk into a monster to strike it with weapon
  • Walk into gold (*) or items to pick them up
  • s : Search adjacent tiles for secret doors/traps

Item & Pack Commands

  • i : Inventory & equipment status
  • e : Eat food
  • q : Quaff (drink) a potion
  • r : Read a scroll
  • z : Zap a wand or staff
  • w / W : Wield weapon / Wear armor
  • T : Take off armor
  • P / R : Put on ring / Remove ring
  • d : Drop item from pack

Dungeon & System

  • > : Descend staircase to next level
  • < : Ascend staircase (requires Amulet on L26)
  • Shift+Q : Quit quest & record score
  • Space : Dismiss --More-- messages
  • Ctrl+L : Level selector / jump dialog
